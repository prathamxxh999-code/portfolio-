About

I'm a product designer with 3+ years of experience in UI, UX and design systems for enterprise B2B products. My work spans banking, finance, hospitality and facility management, where complex workflows need to feel simple.

I've designed core banking and lending platforms for Indian banking environments, along with SaaS tools for gate passes, visitors, parking, assets and support. I start with structure, then refine the details: clear hierarchy, consistent components and screens that make the next step obvious.
